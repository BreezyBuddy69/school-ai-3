# English Grammar — Key Rules

## Tenses Overview

### Simple Tenses
| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| Simple Present | V / V+s | habits, facts | She works every day. |
| Simple Past | V+ed / irregular | completed actions | He went home. |
| Simple Future | will + V | decisions, predictions | It will rain. |

### Continuous / Progressive
| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | happening now | I am studying. |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | interrupted action | I was sleeping when... |
| Future Continuous | will be + V-ing | ongoing future action | I will be waiting. |

### Perfect Tenses
| Tense | Form | Use | Example |
|-------|------|-----|---------|
| Present Perfect | have/has + past participle | experience, recent past | I have seen that film. |
| Past Perfect | had + past participle | before another past event | She had left before he arrived. |
| Future Perfect | will have + past participle | completed before future | By June, I will have finished. |

## Conditional Sentences
| Type | Condition | Result | Example |
|------|-----------|--------|---------|
| 0 | Simple Present | Simple Present | If water boils, it becomes steam. |
| 1 | Simple Present | will + V | If it rains, I will stay home. |
| 2 | Simple Past | would + V | If I had time, I would help. |
| 3 | Past Perfect | would have + PP | If I had known, I would have helped. |

## Passive Voice
**Active:** The teacher corrects the test.
**Passive:** The test is corrected (by the teacher).

Structure: **to be + past participle**
- Present: is/are + PP
- Past: was/were + PP
- Perfect: has/have been + PP

## Reported Speech
Direct: She said, "I am tired."
Reported: She said (that) she was tired.

**Tense backshift:**
- am/is → was
- will → would
- can → could
- have → had

## Relative Clauses
- **who** = people
- **which** = things
- **that** = people or things (informal)
- **whose** = possession
- **where** = place

Defining: no commas — "The man **who** called is my uncle."
Non-defining: commas — "My uncle, **who** lives in Zurich, called me."
