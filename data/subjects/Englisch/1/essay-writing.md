# Essay Writing in English

## Essay Structure

### Introduction
1. **Hook**: Start with an interesting statement, question, or statistic
2. **Background**: Brief context on the topic
3. **Thesis statement**: Your main argument / position

### Body Paragraphs (each paragraph = one idea)
**PEEL structure:**
- **P**oint — main claim of paragraph
- **E**vidence — example, quote, statistic
- **E**xplanation — how the evidence supports your point
- **L**ink — connect back to thesis or to next paragraph

### Conclusion
1. Restate thesis (in different words)
2. Summarize main points briefly
3. Final thought / call to action / broader implication

## Types of Essays

### Argumentative / Persuasive
- Take a clear position
- Use evidence and logic
- Address counter-arguments

### Discursive (For and Against)
- Present both sides fairly
- Use balancing language
- Conclusion: your opinion or balanced summary

### Analytical
- Break down a text/topic
- Examine how/why something works
- Use evidence from the text

## Useful Linking Words

**Adding:** Furthermore, Moreover, In addition, Also
**Contrasting:** However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, Although
**Cause/Effect:** Therefore, Consequently, As a result, Due to
**Examples:** For instance, For example, Such as, Namely
**Conclusion:** In conclusion, To sum up, Overall, In summary

## Formal Language Rules
- ❌ No contractions (don't → do not, it's → it is)
- ❌ No slang or informal expressions
- ✓ Use precise vocabulary
- ✓ Vary sentence length
- ✓ Active voice preferred (passive acceptable for variety)
- ✓ Third person (avoid "I think" in analytical essays)
