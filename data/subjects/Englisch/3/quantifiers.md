# Quantifiers — much, many, a lot of, a few, a little, some, any

> ✅ Aus deinen echten OneNote-Notizen (Jahr 3) übernommen und aufbereitet.

## Zählbar (countable) vs. nicht zählbar (uncountable)

| | Zählbar (Plural) | Nicht zählbar |
|---|---|---|
| **grosse Menge** | **many** / a lot of / lots of | **much** / a lot of / lots of |
| **kleine Menge** | **a few** | **a little** |

> Merke: *a lot of* / *lots of* funktionieren bei **beiden** Nomen-Arten — dafür braucht man sich nicht zu entscheiden. **much/many** stehen meist in **Fragen und Verneinungen**, in **bejahten Aussagen** klingt *a lot of* natürlicher.

## much / many

| Regel | Beispiel |
|---|---|
| much/many in Fragen (?) und Verneinungen (–) | *Did they invite many people?* / *There isn't much coffee.* |
| **too much/many** in bejahten Sätzen | *There is too much salt in the soup.* |
| **how much/many** für Mengenfragen | *How many children do you have?* |

## a lot of / lots of

| Regel | Beispiel |
|---|---|
| a lot/lots of + Nomen | *He spends a lot of time watching TV.* |
| a lot/lots **ohne** of am Satzende | *I like her a lot.* |

## few/little vs. a few/a little

> Achtung Prüfung: Der Artikel **a** macht den Unterschied!

| Ohne „a" = fast keine/negativ | Mit „a" = wenig, aber genug/positiv |
|---|---|
| **little** — *There's little milk in the fridge. We need to buy more.* | **a little** — *I speak a little English.* (reicht aus) |
| **few** — *Few people know this.* | **a few** — *There are a few beers in the fridge.* (genug für jetzt) |

## some / any

| Regel | Beispiel |
|---|---|
| **some** in bejahten Sätzen (+) | *I have some questions for you.* |
| **any** in Verneinungen (–) und Fragen (?) | *Is there any sugar? We didn't have any problems.* |
| **some** in Fragen, wenn man **anbietet oder bittet** | *Would you like some tea? Can I have some tea?* |

### Zusammensetzungen (Compounds)

| some- (+/anbieten) | any- (–/?) |
|---|---|
| somebody, something, somewhere | anybody, anything, anywhere |

> Beispiel: *I saw somebody round the corner.* / *Peter didn't see anybody.* / *Have you got anything to drink?*

## Häufige Fehler

- ❌ *much* in bejahten Sätzen: ~~I have much money.~~ → *I have **a lot of** money.*
- ❌ *little/few* statt *a little/a few* wenn „genug" gemeint ist: ~~I speak little English (= kaum)~~ vs. *I speak **a little** English (= etwas, reicht).*
- ❌ *some* in Verneinungen: ~~I don't have some time.~~ → *I don't have **any** time.*
