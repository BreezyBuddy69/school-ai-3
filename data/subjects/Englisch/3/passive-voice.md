# Passive Voice

> ✅ Aus deinen echten OneNote-Notizen (Jahr 3) übernommen und aufbereitet.

## Aktiv vs. Passiv

Im Aktivsatz steht das **Subjekt**, das die Handlung ausführt, an erster Stelle. Im Passiv wird stattdessen das **Objekt** der Handlung zum Subjekt — wer/was die Handlung ausführt (der **Agent**), ist oft unbekannt, unwichtig oder wird mit **by** angehängt.

| Aktiv | Passiv |
|---|---|
| *Apple **produces** the iPhone.* | *The iPhone **is produced** by Apple.* |
| *They **built** this house in 1930.* | *This house **was built** in 1930.* |

> Merke: Passiv wird oft benutzt, wenn der Fokus auf der **Handlung/dem Ergebnis** liegt, nicht darauf, **wer** sie ausgeführt hat: *The London Underground system is used (by millions of people).*

## Form: to be + past participle

**Passiv = eine Form von „to be" (in der passenden Zeit) + 3. Verbform (past participle)**

| Zeit | Aktiv (Beispiel) | Passiv |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | They make cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| Past Simple | They built this house in 1930. | This house **was built** in 1930. |
| Present Perfect | Someone has cleaned the room. | The room **has been cleaned**. |
| Future (will) | They will finish the project. | The project **will be finished**. |

## Present Simple Passiv — Beispiele

> *Most of the things we buy **are made** in China these days.*
> *I study Art — it **is taught** in my school.*
> *English **is spoken** here. (produce) Wine **is produced** in England.*
> *A lot of patience **is needed** in this job. (need)*
> *These programmes **are watched** by people all over the world. (watch)*
> *The artist sings three songs. **Are** three songs **sung** by the artist?*

## Past Simple Passiv — Beispiele

> *Lamps and candles **were used** for light, because there was no electricity.* (use)
> *A coach instead of a car **was bought** to move from place to place.* (buy)
> *By the 1800s, bicycles **were ridden** along with horses.* (ride)
> *A simple small house **was built** by every family.* (build)
> *This country doesn't produce halloumi. → Halloumi **isn't produced** in this country.*
> *They didn't give me the results of my test. → The results of my test **weren't given** to me.*

## Fragen im Passiv

> *Doesn't Stephan set the timer? — No, the timer **isn't set** by Stephan.*
> *Do my friends find my keys? — Yes, my keys **are found** by my friends.*

## Häufige Fehler

- ❌ Passiv ohne **to be**: ~~The book wrote by the author.~~ → *The book **was written** by the author.*
- ❌ falsche Verbform statt Past Participle: ~~The house was build in 1990.~~ → *The house was **built** in 1990.*
- ❌ Aktiv-Objekt und Passiv-Subjekt verwechseln: das **Objekt** des Aktivsatzes wird zum **Subjekt** des Passivsatzes, nicht umgekehrt.
