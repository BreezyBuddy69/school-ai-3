# Past Simple & Past Continuous

## Past Simple — Form

| | Aussage | Verneinung | Frage |
|---|---|---|---|
| alle Personen | Grundform + **-ed** (regelmässig) / eigene Form (unregelmässig) | **didn't** + Grundform | **Did** + Subjekt + Grundform? |
| *to be* | was / were | wasn't / weren't | Was/Were …? |

> Achtung Prüfung: Bei **didn't** + Grundform steht das Verb wieder im **Infinitiv ohne -ed**: ~~He didn't lived there.~~ → *He **didn't live** there.* — dasselbe gilt für Fragen: ~~Did you went?~~ → *Did you **go**?*

### -ed-Aussprache/Schreibung (regelmässige Verben)
- Verb endet auf Konsonant + **-e** → nur **+ d** (*live → lived*)
- Verb endet auf **Konsonant-Vokal-Konsonant** (betont) → Endkonsonant **verdoppeln + ed** (*stop → stopped*)
- Verb endet auf **Konsonant + y** → **y → i + ed** (*study → studied*)

### Wichtige unregelmässige Verben (Auswahl)
| Infinitiv | Past Simple | Past Participle |
|---|---|---|
| go | went | gone |
| see | saw | seen |
| do | did | done |
| have | had | had |
| write | wrote | written |
| take | took | taken |
| break | broke | broken |
| begin | began | begun |
| catch | caught | caught |
| come | came | come |
| drive | drove | driven |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| fall | fell | fallen |
| know | knew | known |

## Past Continuous — Form

**was/were + Grundform + -ing**

## Wofür Past Simple?

| Gebrauch | Beispiel |
|---|---|
| **Finished time word** (abgeschlossener Zeitpunkt) | *I went to the cinema yesterday.* (yesterday, last night, in 2003, ago) |
| **Finished time period** | *Leonardo painted the Mona Lisa.* |
| **Details of news** | *I've hurt my leg. I fell off a ladder.* (Detail zu einer Present-Perfect-Neuigkeit) |
| **Actions in stories** | *He sat down and ordered a coffee.* (Handlungsfolge — Hintergrund dazu: Past Continuous) |
| **Unreal/imaginary things** | *If I won the lottery, I would buy a house.* (wish, it's time, second conditional) |

## Wofür Past Continuous?

| Gebrauch | Beispiel |
|---|---|
| **Overlapping action** (unterbrochene Handlung) | *I was walking to the station when I met John.* — Use with: **when** + Past Simple |
| **Story background** | *The birds were singing and the sun was shining. Amy sat down.* |
| **Some past habits** | *She was constantly forgetting things.* — Use with: always, constantly |
| **Emphasis of length of action** | *I was working in the garden all day.* — Use with: all day, all evening, for hours |

> Merke: **Past Continuous + when + Past Simple** = eine kürzere Handlung (Past Simple) unterbricht eine längere, bereits laufende Handlung (Past Continuous): *Tom was watching TV when the telephone rang.*

## Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

> Achtung Prüfung: **at three o'clock yesterday** / **when I was at work** → Past Continuous, weil es um eine Handlung **zu einem bestimmten Zeitpunkt in der Vergangenheit** geht, nicht um deren Abschluss.

## Häufige Fehler

- ❌ *-ed* nach **did/didn't**: ~~She didn't worked.~~ → *She **didn't work**.*
- ❌ Past Continuous ohne *to be*: ~~I working when she called.~~ → *I **was working** when she called.*
- ❌ Zeitadverbien am Satzende bei komplexeren Sätzen: **Time adverbs go in the middle** (z. B. *I have never been to Spain*, nicht *I have been never to Spain*).
